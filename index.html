Selling with Larkline

No volume game. A plan for your home.

The big portals show buyers hundreds of homes. We do the opposite: we help one seller at a time make good decisions.

  1. 01

    A valuation visit

    Your agent walks through your home, then brings back a price based on recent sales in your street, not a portal average.

  2. 02

    A written plan for your home

    Asking price, photos, the buyers we will look for and the right moment to list. You always know what happens next.

  3. 03

    Viewings with your own agent

    The person who knows your home is the one who shows it. No junior, no call centre.

  4. 04

    All the way to the notary

    We negotiate the offer, prepare the sales agreement (compromis de vente) and stay with you until the final signing.

Neighborhood guide

Not sure where to live? Start with the street

Centre historique

Timber-framed houses, Rue Kéréon and the cathedral at the end of almost every street. Apartments above the shops and a few rare lofts. You will not need a car.

Walking everywhere

Locmaria

Where Quimper began. Faience workshops on Place Bérardier, the towpath along the Odet and the steps up Mont Frugy, ten minutes on foot from the cathedral.
