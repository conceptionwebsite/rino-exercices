Who looks after your data

Larkline Estates, Rue René-Madec, 29000 Quimper, is responsible for the personal data collected on this website. For any question, write to Claire Morgan at hello@larkline.example.

What we collect

Only what you give us:

  • Valuation request: the street and neighborhood of your home, its type, size and number of bedrooms, your name, phone number, email address and the best time to call.
  • When you contact an agent by phone, WhatsApp or email: the details you choose to share.

Why we use it

To answer your request: call you back, plan a visit and give you a valuation of your home. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Legal basis: the steps you ask us to take before a possible sales mandate, and our legitimate interest in following up a request you made.

Who sees it

Only the Larkline team, and the companies that host our website and our email. If you write to an agent on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy also applies.

How long we keep it

If no sale follows, we delete your details three years after our last contact with you. Confirm the retention periods.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or ask for a copy. Write to hello@larkline.example. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. List any analytics tool chosen at launch.
