Locmaria or the Moulin Vert: how to choose your neighborhood

Both sit close to the centre of Quimper, but living there feels very different. Here is how we help buyers choose.

  • Daniel Reyes
  • Sofia Lindqvist

In this article

Locmaria Le Moulin Vert Side by side How to decide

Most people who call us already know they want to live in Quimper. The harder question is where. Locmaria and the Moulin Vert come up often, because both are a short trip from the centre and both have homes with character. They suit very different lives, though.

Locmaria: where Quimper began

Locmaria is the oldest part of town, on the left bank of the Odet. Its Romanesque church, Notre-Dame de Locmaria, and the faience workshops around Place Bérardier give the streets their character. The towpath runs along the river, and the steps up Mont Frugy start just behind Rue Haute.

Homes here are mostly older townhouses, apartments in converted buildings and the occasional workshop turned into a loft, like our Cobblestone Loft. Gardens are small or absent, and parking takes patience on the narrow streets.

Le Moulin Vert: space and quiet

The Moulin Vert is a residential part of town west of the centre. The streets are calmer and leafier, and you find more houses with gardens. Families often choose it for the room it gives them.

You will probably use the car or the bike more than in Locmaria, but the centre is still close. Homes range from family houses to larger townhouses, like the Moulin Vert Townhouse Sofia has on sale now.

How to decide

We ask our buyers three questions before we show them anything:

  1. Do you want to walk everywhere? Then Locmaria, or the centre, will make you happy.
  2. Do you need a garden? The Moulin Vert gives you far more choice.
  3. How many cars do you have? Two cars in Locmaria is a daily puzzle.

Then walk both, twice: once on a Saturday morning and once on a weekday evening. A street can feel very different at those two moments, and that is often what decides it.
