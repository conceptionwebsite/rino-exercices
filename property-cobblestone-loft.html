A workshop turned into a light-filled loft

On a cobbled corner of Locmaria, this former workshop has become a loft with ceilings up to four metres, exposed oak beams and a library on the mezzanine.

Three bedrooms sit under the roof, one with its own fireplace. The kitchen opens onto a small paved courtyard, and the river Odet is at the end of the street. You can walk to the cathedral in ten minutes.
